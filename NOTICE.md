CARBINE uses or is designed to use the USDA Forest Service Forest Vegetation Simulator (FVS) as its calculation engine.

FVS is developed and maintained by the USDA Forest Service. CARBINE is not an official USDA Forest Service product, and use of FVS in CARBINE does not imply USDA endorsement.

Users should verify FVS version, selected variant, input data, assumptions, and interpretation of model outputs before relying on results.
