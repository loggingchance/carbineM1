# CARBINE Product Brief

CARBINE is a compact forest carbon scenario explorer powered by the USDA Forest Service Forest Vegetation Simulator. It helps users upload a stand inventory, choose a location or FVS variant, compare management scenarios, and generate readable carbon charts, tables, and reports.

The first implementation focuses on a working static-app spine: local inventory import, validation, typed scenarios, an FVS adapter interface, demo-only results, and report exports. Real carbon results must come from FVS once a browser-compatible runtime is connected.

## Required Positioning

CARBINE is independently developed and is not an official USDA Forest Service product. It is powered by the USDA Forest Service Forest Vegetation Simulator. Users are responsible for reviewing inputs, assumptions, variant selection, and interpretation of outputs.
